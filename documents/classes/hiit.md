# HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training)
*Category: Classes · Last updated: September 2026*

HIIT alternates short bursts of hard work with brief recovery, using bodyweight moves, kettlebells, rowers and assault bikes.

## When and who
- Monday 7:00am, Wednesday 7:00am and Thursday 6:00pm, all with Jordan Lee.
- 45 minutes, Studio 1, maximum 20 people.

## Level
All levels. Jordan gives an easier and a harder option for every exercise. If you are new to exercise or returning from injury, tell Jordan before the class starts.

## What to bring
Water bottle, towel and training shoes with good grip. Mats and equipment are provided.

## Late arrival
The doors close 10 minutes after the start because the warm-up is part of the safety plan. If you arrive later you won't be admitted, and it counts as a no-show.

## Included in
Plus (as your one class of the week) and Elite.
