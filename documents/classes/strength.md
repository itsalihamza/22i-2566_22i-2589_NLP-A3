# Strength
*Category: Classes · Last updated: September 2026*

A coached barbell and dumbbell class teaching the main lifts — squat, deadlift, bench press, overhead press and rows — with progressive weights week to week.

## When and who
- Tuesday 7:00am and Thursday 7:00am with Alex Chen.
- 60 minutes on the lifting platforms, maximum 12 people (one platform per two members).

## Level
Beginners are welcome: Alex starts every newcomer with an empty bar and checks technique before adding weight. Experienced lifters follow a written programme on the board.

## What to bring
Flat-soled shoes, a water bottle and a training log if you have one. Chalk is allowed on the lifting platforms only. Belts and wrist wraps are available to borrow.

## Included in
Plus (as your one class of the week) and Elite.
