# Pilates
*Category: Classes · Last updated: September 2026*

Mat Pilates focused on core control, posture and the small stabilising muscles around the hips and spine. IronPulse runs mat Pilates only — there are no reformer machines.

## When and who
- Tuesday 12:00pm and Friday 12:00pm with Sam Rivera.
- 50 minutes, Studio 1, maximum 15 people (smaller group so Sam can correct form).

## Level
Beginner to intermediate. Many members use it alongside strength training to protect the lower back.

## What to bring
Mats, small balls, rings and resistance bands are provided. Grip socks are recommended; they're sold at the front desk.

## Included in
Plus (as your one class of the week) and Elite.
