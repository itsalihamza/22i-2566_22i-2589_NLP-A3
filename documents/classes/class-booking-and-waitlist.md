# Class Booking, Cancellation and Waitlist
*Category: Classes · Last updated: September 2026*

## Who can book
Plus members (one class per week, Monday–Sunday) and Elite members (unlimited). Basic and Student members cannot book classes.

## How to book
- In the IronPulse member app (Classes tab) or at the front desk.
- Booking opens 7 days before the class at the same time of day, e.g. Monday 7:00am HIIT opens the previous Monday at 7:00am.
- You can hold a maximum of 4 future bookings at once.

## Cancelling
- Cancel at least 2 hours before the class in the app or at the front desk.
- Later cancellations and no-shows count as a used class (for Plus, it is your class for that week).
- Elite members with three no-shows within 30 days cannot book for 7 days.

## Waitlist
- When a class is full you can join the waitlist in the app (maximum 10 people).
- Elite members are placed ahead of Plus members.
- If a spot opens up to 2 hours before the class, the first person is booked automatically and gets a push notification. Within the last 2 hours, spots are offered by notification and the first person to accept gets it.
