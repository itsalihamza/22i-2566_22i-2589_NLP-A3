# Saturday Bootcamp
*Category: Classes · Last updated: September 2026*

An outdoor, team-based conditioning class with sled pushes, sandbag carries, sprints and partner circuits.

## When and who
- Saturday 9:00am with Jordan Lee.
- 60 minutes, outdoors in car park area B (coned off for the class), maximum 25 people.

## Weather
Bootcamp runs in light rain. In heavy rain, lightning or temperatures above 35°C it moves indoors to Studio 1 with a reduced format. Any change is announced in the member app by 7:00am.

## Level
All levels; you work at your own pace within the team.

## What to bring
Outdoor training shoes, water, and layers in cold weather.

## Monthly challenge
On the last Saturday of each month, Bootcamp is the IronPulse Community Challenge (see Community Events).

## Included in
Plus (as your one class of the week) and Elite.
