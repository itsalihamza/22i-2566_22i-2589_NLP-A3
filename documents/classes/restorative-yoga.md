# Restorative Yoga
*Category: Classes · Last updated: September 2026*

A slow, calming class built around long, supported stretches held for several minutes, with breathing exercises. It is the gentlest class on the IronPulse timetable.

## When and who
- Sunday 10:00am with Sam Rivera.
- 75 minutes, Studio 1, maximum 25 people.

## Level
Suitable for everyone, including complete beginners and members recovering from hard training weeks.

## What to bring
Bolsters, blankets, blocks and mats are provided. Wear warm, comfortable clothing — the studio lights are dimmed and the room is kept cooler than in other classes.

## Good to know
Late entry is not allowed after 10:15am so the class can stay quiet.

## Included in
Plus (as your one class of the week) and Elite.
