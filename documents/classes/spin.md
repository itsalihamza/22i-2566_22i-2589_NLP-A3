# Spin
*Category: Classes · Last updated: September 2026*

An indoor cycling class set to music, with climbs, sprints and intervals. You control the resistance, so it suits every fitness level.

## When and who
- Monday 6:00pm and Friday 7:00am with Jordan Lee.
- 45 minutes in the Spin Studio, which has 18 bikes — so the class is limited to 18 people.

## Level
All levels. First-timers should arrive 10 minutes early so Jordan can set up the bike (seat height, handlebars, cleats).

## What to bring
A water bottle and a small towel. Bikes take standard SPD cleats; trainers also work with the toe cages. Padded shorts are optional.

## Late arrival
Doors close 10 minutes after the start. Late arrivals are not admitted, and it counts as a no-show.

## Included in
Plus (as your one class of the week) and Elite.
