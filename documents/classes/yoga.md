# Yoga
*Category: Classes · Last updated: September 2026*

A flowing vinyasa-style class that builds flexibility, balance and core strength, finishing with a short relaxation.

## When and who
- Monday 12:00pm and Thursday 12:00pm with Sam Rivera.
- 60 minutes, Studio 1, maximum 25 people.

## Level
All levels; beginners are welcome. Sam shows modifications for tight hamstrings, wrists and lower backs.

## What to bring
Mats, blocks and straps are provided and cleaned after every class. You may bring your own mat. Train barefoot; leave shoes in the cubbies outside the studio.

## Good to know
- Arrive a few minutes early to set up quietly; late entry is allowed up to 10 minutes after the start.
- Pregnant members should get approval from their doctor first and tell Sam before class.
- For a slower, deeper stretch, see Restorative Yoga on Sundays.

## Included in
Plus (as your one class of the week) and Elite.
