# Core
*Category: Classes · Last updated: September 2026*

A short, focused class for the abdominals, obliques and lower back, using planks, carries, cable work and stability-ball exercises.

## When and who
- Friday 5:00pm with Alex Chen.
- 30 minutes, Studio 1, maximum 20 people.

## Level
All levels. It's a popular add-on before or after a gym-floor workout.

## Good to know
Because it is only 30 minutes, it still counts as one full class for Plus members' weekly allowance.

## Included in
Plus (as your one class of the week) and Elite.
