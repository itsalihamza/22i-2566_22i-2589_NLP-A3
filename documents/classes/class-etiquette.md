# Class Etiquette and First-Class Tips
*Category: Classes · Last updated: September 2026*

## Before class
- Arrive 5–10 minutes early; first-timers should introduce themselves to the instructor and mention any injuries.
- Check in on the studio tablet or the instructor will mark you absent.
- Phones on silent and kept in the cubbies, not on the floor.

## Late entry rules
- HIIT and Spin: doors close 10 minutes after the start (safety).
- Restorative Yoga: no entry after 10:15am.
- All other classes: late entry up to 10 minutes after the start, quietly at the back.

## During and after class
- Follow the instructor's safety cues and take the easier option if anything hurts.
- Wipe down and return all equipment — mats, bells and bands go back to the marked shelves.
- Leave the studio promptly; the next class may need a set-up window.

## Feedback
Class feedback can be left in the app after each session; instructors read it weekly.
