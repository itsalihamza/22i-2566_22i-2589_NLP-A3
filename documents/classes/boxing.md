# Boxing
*Category: Classes · Last updated: September 2026*

A non-contact boxing fitness class: footwork, combinations on pads and heavy bags, plus conditioning rounds. There is no sparring.

## When and who
- Wednesday 6:00pm with Alex Chen.
- 60 minutes, Studio 1, maximum 16 people.

## Level
All levels. Alex teaches the basic stance and punches at the start of every class.

## What to bring
- Boxing gloves can be borrowed free from the studio.
- Hand wraps are personal for hygiene reasons: bring your own or buy a pair at the front desk for $8.
- Water bottle and towel.

## Included in
Plus (as your one class of the week) and Elite.
