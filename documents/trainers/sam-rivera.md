# Trainer Bio: Sam Rivera
*Category: Trainers · Last updated: September 2026*

Sam Rivera runs IronPulse's mind-body classes: Yoga, Pilates and Restorative Yoga.

## Background
- 500-hour registered yoga teacher (RYT-500) and certified mat Pilates instructor.
- Previously a dancer; specialises in mobility, flexibility and posture.
- Joined IronPulse in 2020.

## Classes Sam teaches
- Yoga — Monday and Thursday 12:00pm
- Pilates — Tuesday and Friday 12:00pm
- Restorative Yoga — Sunday 10:00am

## Personal training
Sam offers one-to-one mobility and Pilates sessions on weekdays from 1:00pm to 4:00pm, at the standard personal-training prices.

## Coaching style
Calm and detailed. Sam is especially popular with desk workers who have stiff hips and shoulders, and with lifters who want to improve their squat depth.
