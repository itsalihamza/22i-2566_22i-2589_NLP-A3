# Free Intro Session
*Category: Trainers · Last updated: September 2026*

Every new IronPulse member gets one free 30-minute intro session with a trainer.

## What happens in the session
- A short chat about your goals, training history and any injuries.
- A tour of the gym floor and how the main machines work.
- A simple starter workout plan written for you, saved in the member app.

## Rules
- One free session per new member, on any plan (Basic, Student, Plus or Elite).
- It must be used within your first 30 days of membership; it does not carry over after that.
- Book it at the front desk (it cannot be booked in the app). You can ask for a specific trainer, subject to availability.
- If you can't make it, cancel at least 24 hours ahead to rebook; a no-show uses up the free session.

## After the intro
Many members continue with a personal training pack; there is no obligation to buy one.
