# Trainer Bio: Jordan Lee
*Category: Trainers · Last updated: September 2026*

Jordan Lee leads IronPulse's conditioning programme: HIIT, Spin and the Saturday Bootcamp.

## Background
- Former college 400m sprinter.
- Certified personal trainer and indoor cycling instructor; first-aid and CPR instructor for the IronPulse staff team.
- Joined IronPulse in 2021.

## Classes Jordan teaches
- HIIT — Monday 7:00am, Wednesday 7:00am, Thursday 6:00pm
- Spin — Monday 6:00pm, Friday 7:00am
- Bootcamp — Saturday 9:00am (and the monthly Community Challenge)

## Personal training
Jordan takes personal training clients on Tuesday and Thursday evenings (4:00pm–8:00pm), focusing on fat loss, running fitness and general conditioning.

## Coaching style
High energy, music-driven, and always gives a lower-impact option for every move so no one is left out.
