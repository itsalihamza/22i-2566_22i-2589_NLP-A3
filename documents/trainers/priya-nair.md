# Trainer Bio: Priya Nair
*Category: Trainers · Last updated: September 2026*

Priya Nair is a personal trainer at IronPulse Fitness. She does not teach group classes; she works one-to-one and with pairs.

## Background
- BSc in Sport and Exercise Science.
- Certified personal trainer with a specialist certificate in post-rehabilitation exercise.
- Joined IronPulse in 2023.

## Specialities
- Returning to training after an injury, once a physiotherapist or doctor has cleared you.
- Pre- and post-natal exercise (with medical clearance).
- First-time gym users who want a structured 6-week starter programme.

## Availability
Monday, Wednesday and Friday 4:00pm–9:00pm, and Saturday 10:00am–2:00pm.

## Important
Priya does not diagnose or treat injuries. If something hurts, see a doctor or physiotherapist first; Priya then designs training around their advice.
