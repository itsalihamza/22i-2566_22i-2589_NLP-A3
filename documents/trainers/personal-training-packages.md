# Personal Training Packages
*Category: Trainers · Last updated: September 2026*

One-to-one coaching with any IronPulse trainer: Alex Chen, Jordan Lee, Sam Rivera, Priya Nair or Marcus Webb. Personal training is available to members on every plan; it is not included in any membership.

## Prices
- Single 60-minute session: $55.
- 5-session pack: $250 ($50 per session), valid for 3 months from purchase.
- 10-session pack: $460 ($46 per session), valid for 6 months from purchase.
- Partner training (two members together with one trainer): add $20 per session.

## Rules
- Book and pay at the front desk; the trainer confirms the time.
- Cancel at least 24 hours in advance, or the session is charged / deducted from your pack.
- Unused sessions expire at the end of the pack's validity and are not refunded.
- Packs can be paused while your membership is frozen; the validity is extended by the frozen time.

## First step
New members should use their free 30-minute intro session first (see Free Intro Session) to meet a trainer before buying a pack.
