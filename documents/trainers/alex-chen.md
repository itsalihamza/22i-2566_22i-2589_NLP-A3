# Trainer Bio: Alex Chen
*Category: Trainers · Last updated: September 2026*

Alex Chen is IronPulse's head coach for strength and boxing, and has coached at IronPulse since it opened in 2019.

## Background
- Former amateur boxer (regional middleweight finalist, 2014).
- Certified Strength and Conditioning Specialist (CSCS) and Level 2 boxing coach.
- Specialises in teaching beginners the main barbell lifts safely and in strength training for members over 50.

## Classes Alex teaches
- Strength — Tuesday and Thursday 7:00am
- Boxing — Wednesday 6:00pm
- Core — Friday 5:00pm

## Personal training
Alex takes personal training clients on Monday, Wednesday and Friday mornings (8:00am–12:00pm). Sessions are booked through the front desk at the standard personal-training prices.

## Coaching style
Patient and technical: "Lift well first, lift heavy later." Alex films a short technique video for new strength members so they can review their form at home.
