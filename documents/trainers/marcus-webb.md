# Trainer Bio: Marcus Webb
*Category: Trainers · Last updated: September 2026*

Marcus Webb is a personal trainer at IronPulse Fitness who specialises in powerlifting and muscle building. He works one-to-one only and does not teach group classes.

## Background
- Competitive powerlifter (national masters bronze medallist, 2022).
- Certified personal trainer and powerlifting coach.
- Joined IronPulse in 2022.

## Specialities
- Squat, bench press and deadlift technique and programming.
- Competition preparation, including meet-day planning.
- Muscle-building programmes for intermediate lifters.

## Availability
Tuesday and Thursday 6:00am–12:00pm, and Sunday 9:00am–1:00pm.

## Good to know
Marcus runs a free 20-minute "Lifting Technique Check" for any member on the first Sunday of each month at 12:00pm, first come first served at the lifting platforms.
