# New Member FAQ
*Category: FAQ · Last updated: September 2026*

## Is there a contract?
No minimum contract on any plan. You can cancel with 30 days' written notice.

## Can I change plans later?
Yes. Upgrades start immediately (you pay the difference for the rest of the month); downgrades start from the next billing month.

## What if I don't know how to use the equipment?
Book your free 30-minute intro session in your first 30 days. Staff on the gym floor can also show you any machine during staffed hours.

## Can I bring a friend?
Yes, during staffed hours: Elite members get 2 free guest passes a month; otherwise a guest pays a $10 guest day pass.

## Can I pause my membership?
Yes — freeze for up to 3 months per year at $5 per month (request at least 7 days ahead), or a free medical freeze of up to 6 months with a doctor's note.

## Where do I park?
Free for 2 hours in the Ridgeway Plaza car park; validate your ticket at the front desk.

## Who do I contact?
The front desk on (555) 014-2290 during staffed hours, or hello@ironpulsefitness.example.
