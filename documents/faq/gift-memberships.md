# Gift Memberships
*Category: FAQ · Last updated: September 2026*

## What can I buy as a gift?
- A 1-month Plus gift card: $49.
- A 1-month Elite gift card: $79.
- A personal training 5-session pack: $250.
Gift cards for the Basic and Student plans are not sold.

## Rules
- Gift cards are bought at the front desk and printed on the spot or emailed.
- They must be activated within 12 months of purchase. The 1-month membership starts on the activation day.
- The person receiving the gift doesn't pay the $20 joining fee for the gift month.
- At the end of the gift month the membership simply stops; it only continues if the recipient signs up and adds a payment method.
- Gift cards can't be exchanged for cash or used to pay an existing member's monthly fee.

## Can existing members receive one?
Existing members can use a personal training gift pack, but not a membership gift card.
