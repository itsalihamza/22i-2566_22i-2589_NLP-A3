# Group Classes FAQ
*Category: FAQ · Last updated: September 2026*

## Which plans include classes?
Plus includes one class per week (Monday–Sunday) and Elite includes unlimited classes. Basic and Student members cannot book classes; there is no pay-per-class option.

## Which class should I start with?
- New to exercise: Yoga, Pilates, Core or Restorative Yoga.
- Want to get fitter fast: HIIT, Spin or Bootcamp.
- Want to get stronger: Strength (Alex starts beginners with an empty bar).
- Want to learn to punch: Boxing (no sparring).

## How far ahead can I book?
Up to 7 days ahead, maximum 4 future bookings at a time.

## What if the class is full?
Join the waitlist in the app. Elite members are placed ahead of Plus members.

## What if I'm late?
HIIT and Spin doors close 10 minutes after the start; Restorative Yoga has no entry after 10:15am; other classes allow late entry up to 10 minutes after the start.

## Are there classes on public holidays?
No. There are no group classes on public holidays.
