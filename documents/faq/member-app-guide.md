# Member App Guide
*Category: FAQ · Last updated: September 2026*

The IronPulse app is free on iOS and Android. Log in with the email address on your membership.

## Main features
- **My card:** your entry QR code (works at the door and the car park exit).
- **Classes:** see the schedule, book (Plus and Elite), cancel, and join waitlists.
- **Account:** change plan, update your payment card, view receipts, and request a freeze with the Freeze form.
- **Messages:** send a message to the front desk; replies within 1 working day.
- **Report:** report faulty equipment or a conduct issue (can be anonymous).
- **Info:** opening hours, the monthly Wi-Fi password and event notices.

## What the app can't do
- Cancel your membership (use the front desk form or email membership@ironpulsefitness.example).
- Book personal training or the free intro session (book at the front desk).

## Problems
If the app won't log in, use "Forgot password". If your QR code doesn't open the door, check your membership status under Account or ask at the front desk.
