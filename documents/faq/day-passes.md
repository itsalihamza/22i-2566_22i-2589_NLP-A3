# Day Passes for Non-Members
*Category: FAQ · Last updated: September 2026*

## Non-member day pass — $15
- For visitors who want to train without a membership and without a member.
- Valid for one day during staffed hours only (Monday–Friday 6:00am–10:00pm, Saturday–Sunday 8:00am–8:00pm).
- Includes the gym floor, changing rooms and showers. It does not include classes or the sauna.
- Photo ID and a health questionnaire are required; visitors must be 18+ (16–17 with a guardian present to sign the waiver).
- Maximum 4 non-member day passes per person per calendar month.

## Guest day pass — $10
For someone training with a member (see the Guest Policy). Elite members get 2 free guest passes per month.

## Does a day pass count towards joining?
If you join within 7 days of buying a non-member day pass, the $15 is taken off your joining fee.

## Where to buy
At the front desk only, by card or cash.
