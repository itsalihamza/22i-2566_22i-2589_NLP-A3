# Should I Freeze or Cancel?
*Category: FAQ · Last updated: September 2026*

## I'm travelling for a month or two
Freeze. A standard freeze costs $5 per month, lasts up to 3 months per year, and keeps your current price and personal locker. Request it at least 7 days before it starts.

## I'm injured or ill
Ask for a medical freeze: up to 6 months, no fee, with a doctor's or physiotherapist's note. No 7-day notice needed.

## I'm moving away or want to stop for good
Cancel with 30 days' written notice (front desk form or email). There is no refund for the current month, and the $20 joining fee applies if you rejoin later.

## I can't afford it right now
You could downgrade (for example Elite to Plus, or Plus to Basic) from the next billing month instead of cancelling, or freeze for up to 3 months at $5 per month.

## I joined in the last 7 days and changed my mind
Use the cooling-off period: a full refund of the first month's fee if you have visited no more than 2 times (the joining fee isn't refunded).
