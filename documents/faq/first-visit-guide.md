# Your First Visit
*Category: FAQ · Last updated: September 2026*

## Before you come
- Download the IronPulse app and log in — your entry QR code is under "My card".
- Pack training shoes (closed-toe), a water bottle, a towel (or rent one for $2) and a padlock for the day lockers.

## When you arrive
1. Scan your card or QR code at the entrance.
2. Say hello at the front desk: we'll show you the changing rooms and help you book your free 30-minute intro session (it must be used within your first 30 days).
3. Set up your profile photo if you haven't yet.

## On the gym floor
- Ask any staff member in an IronPulse t-shirt for help with equipment.
- Wipe down equipment after use and re-rack your weights.
- Quietest times to learn the ropes: weekdays 10:00am–3:00pm.

## Your first class
If your plan includes classes (Plus or Elite), arrive 10 minutes early and tell the instructor it's your first time.
