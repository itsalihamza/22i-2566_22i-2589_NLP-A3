# Gym Tours
*Category: FAQ · Last updated: September 2026*

## Do I need to book a tour?
No. Free tours are available any time during staffed hours (Monday–Friday 6:00am–10:00pm, Saturday–Sunday 8:00am–8:00pm), no appointment needed. Just ask at the front desk.

## What does a tour include?
About 15 minutes: the gym floor, studios, recovery zone, changing rooms and (from the doorway) the sauna. You can watch the last few minutes of a class if one is running.

## Can I train on the day of my tour?
Not on the tour itself. To train the same day, buy a $15 non-member day pass, or join and start straight away.

## Can I bring someone?
Yes, friends and family are welcome on a tour. Under-16s can join the tour with a parent but cannot use the equipment.

## Group or accessibility tours
For groups of more than 4, corporate visits, or an accessibility-focused tour, email hello@ironpulsefitness.example to arrange a time.
