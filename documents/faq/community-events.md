# Community Events
*Category: FAQ · Last updated: September 2026*

## Monthly Community Challenge
- On the last Saturday of each month, the 9:00am Bootcamp becomes the IronPulse Community Challenge, led by Jordan Lee.
- Teams of four complete a timed circuit; results go on the leaderboard in the reception lounge.
- Open to Plus and Elite members (it counts as a class booking). Members can bring one guest using a guest pass or a $10 guest day pass.

## Lifting Technique Check
Free 20-minute technique checks with Marcus Webb on the first Sunday of each month at 12:00pm, at the lifting platforms. Open to all members; first come, first served.

## New Member Social
Every second Thursday of the month at 7:30pm in the reception lounge: meet the trainers and other new members. Free, no booking needed.

## Charity Spin-a-thon
Once a year in November, a 6-hour relay on the spin bikes raises money for a local charity. Details are announced in the app in October.
