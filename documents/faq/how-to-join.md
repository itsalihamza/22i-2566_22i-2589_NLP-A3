# How to Join IronPulse
*Category: FAQ · Last updated: September 2026*

## Where can I sign up?
At the front desk during staffed hours, or in the IronPulse member app (Join tab). Corporate and Student memberships must be completed at the front desk because we need to check your ID.

## What do I need?
- Photo ID (and a student ID for the Student plan, or your work email and staff ID for corporate membership).
- A bank account for direct debit or a debit/credit card.
- About 10 minutes to complete the health questionnaire (PAR-Q).
- 16–17 year olds need a parent or guardian with them to sign the waiver.

## What do I pay on the first day?
The $20 joining fee plus the rest of the current month, worked out per day. Full monthly billing starts on the 1st.

## Can I try before I join?
Yes: book a free tour any time during staffed hours, or buy a $15 non-member day pass.

## When can I start?
Straight away. Your access card is printed at the front desk, or use the QR code in the app.
