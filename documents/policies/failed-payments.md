# Failed Payments
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

## What happens if a payment fails
1. Day 0: the monthly payment fails. You get an email and an app notification. There is no fee yet.
2. Day 3: we retry the payment automatically.
3. If the retry also fails, a $10 failed payment fee is added and your access card is paused until the balance is paid. You can pay at the front desk or in the app at any time.
4. Day 14: if the balance is still unpaid, the membership is suspended and class bookings are cancelled.
5. Day 30: an unpaid membership is cancelled and the balance is passed to our collections partner.

## Avoiding a failed payment
Keep your card details up to date in the app. If you know a payment will fail, contact billing@ironpulsefitness.example before the 1st — we can move the payment date by up to 5 days once per year.

## Fee waivers
The $10 fee is only removed if the failure was caused by an IronPulse or bank system error.
