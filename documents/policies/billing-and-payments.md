# Billing and Payments
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

## When you are billed
- Memberships are billed monthly on the 1st of each month.
- If you join mid-month, your first payment covers the rest of that month, worked out per day, plus the $20 joining fee. Full monthly billing starts on the next 1st.

## How you pay
- Direct debit from a bank account, or a debit/credit card stored on your account.
- Update your card in the member app (Account → Payment method) or at the front desk.
- IronPulse does not accept cash for monthly memberships.

## Receipts and invoices
Receipts are emailed after every payment and available in the app under Account → Payments. Company invoices for corporate members are issued on request.

## Price changes
We give at least 30 days' notice by email before any price change. Frozen memberships keep the price they had before the freeze.

## Questions
Billing questions: billing@ironpulsefitness.example or the front desk during staffed hours. For missed payments, see the Failed Payments policy.
