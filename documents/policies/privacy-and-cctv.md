# Privacy and CCTV
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

## What we store
Your name, contact details, date of birth, photo, plan and payment details, entry log, class bookings and health questionnaire answers.

## How it is used
Only to run your membership: access control, billing, bookings and safety. We never sell member data. Marketing emails are opt-in and can be turned off in the app.

## How long we keep it
- Entry logs and booking history: 2 years.
- Health questionnaire and doctor's notes: deleted 12 months after the membership ends (doctor's notes for medical freezes are deleted when the freeze ends).
- CCTV footage: 30 days, unless needed for an incident investigation.

## CCTV
Cameras cover the entrance, gym floor, studios, car park and corridors. There are no cameras in changing rooms, showers or the sauna.

## Your rights
You can request a copy of your data, a correction or deletion by emailing privacy@ironpulsefitness.example. We reply within 30 days.
