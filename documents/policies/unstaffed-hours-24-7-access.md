# 24/7 Access and Unstaffed Hours
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

Plus and Elite members (aged 18+) can enter the gym at any time with their access card or app QR code. Outside staffed hours the gym is unstaffed.

## Staffed and unstaffed times
- Staffed: Monday–Friday 6:00am–10:00pm, Saturday–Sunday 8:00am–8:00pm.
- Unstaffed: all other times. The front desk, studios and sauna are closed; the gym floor, changing rooms and showers stay open.

## Safety during unstaffed hours
- Help points with an emergency button are by the entrance, the lifting platforms and the changing rooms. They connect to a 24-hour monitoring centre that can call emergency services.
- CCTV covers all areas except changing rooms and showers.
- We recommend training with a partner for heavy lifts; use the safety arms in the squat racks.

## Rules
- No guests and no under-18s during unstaffed hours.
- Tailgating (letting someone in behind you) is a serious breach of the Code of Conduct.
- Each entry is logged against your card.

## Not available to
Basic and Student members, whose cards only work during staffed hours.
