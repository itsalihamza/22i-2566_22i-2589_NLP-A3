# Dress Code
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

## On the gym floor and in classes
- Closed-toe athletic shoes must be worn at all times. Sandals, flip-flops, boots and bare feet are not allowed.
- Exceptions: Yoga, Pilates and Restorative Yoga are done barefoot or in grip socks; deadlift slippers and flat lifting shoes are fine on the lifting platforms.
- Wear clean athletic clothing that covers the torso — a sports bra or a vest is fine, no shirtless training.
- No clothing with offensive slogans or images.

## In the sauna
Swimwear or a towel wrap is required; no street clothes or shoes.

## Why
The rules protect your feet from dropped weights, keep equipment hygienic, and make the gym comfortable for everyone. Staff may ask a member to change or to leave the floor if the dress code is not followed.
