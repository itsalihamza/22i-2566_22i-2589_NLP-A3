# Children and Pets
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

## Children
- Under-16s are not allowed on the gym floor, in classes or in the sauna, even with a parent.
- IronPulse has no crèche or childcare service.
- Children may wait in the reception lounge only if supervised by another adult who is not training.
- Members aged 16–17 can join with a guardian's waiver (see the Age Policy).

## Pets
- Pets are not allowed anywhere inside the building.
- Registered assistance dogs are welcome in all areas except the sauna and showers. Please tell the front desk on your first visit.
- Dogs may not be left tied up outside the entrance or in parked cars on hot days.
