# Member Code of Conduct
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

IronPulse should feel welcoming for everyone. All members and guests agree to this code when they join or visit.

## Expected behaviour
- Treat other members and staff with respect. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation or unwanted comments about anyone's body are not tolerated.
- Share equipment: let others "work in" between sets and don't reserve machines with towels or bags.
- Keep noise reasonable — controlled grunting is fine, shouting and slamming weights are not.
- Personal music only with headphones.

## Not allowed
- Bringing food onto the gym floor (sealed drinks and protein shakes in closed bottles are fine).
- Selling products or services, or coaching other members for money (only IronPulse trainers may train clients).
- Letting anyone else in on your access card or QR code, or holding the door open for people during unstaffed hours.

## Consequences
A first minor breach gets a verbal warning. Serious or repeated breaches lead to suspension or cancellation of the membership without refund. Report problems to staff or with the Report form in the app — reports can be anonymous.
