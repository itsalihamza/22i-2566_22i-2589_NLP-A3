# Guest Policy
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

Members can bring a friend to train with them.

## Prices
- Elite members get 2 free guest passes per month. They reset on the 1st and do not roll over.
- All other guests pay a $10 guest day pass at the front desk.
- Someone visiting without a member can buy a $15 non-member day pass instead.

## Rules for guests
- Guests must be 18 or older, or 16–17 with a parent or guardian signing the waiver in person.
- Photo ID is required, and guests complete a short health questionnaire on their first visit.
- A member can bring one guest at a time, and must stay with the guest for the whole visit.
- Guests may only visit during staffed hours (Monday–Friday 6:00am–10:00pm, Saturday–Sunday 8:00am–8:00pm). Guests are never allowed during unstaffed 24/7 hours.
- The same guest can visit at most 3 times per calendar month; after that we ask them to consider joining.

## Classes and sauna
Guests may join a group class only if there is space 2 hours before the start and the member books it for them using the member's own class allowance. Guests of Elite members may use the sauna with the member.
