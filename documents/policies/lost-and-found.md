# Lost and Found
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

## Where lost items go
Items found in the gym, studios and changing rooms are taken to the front desk and logged.

## How long we keep them
- Clothing, bottles and towels: 14 days, then donated to a local charity.
- Valuables (phones, wallets, keys, jewellery, cards): 30 days in the office safe, then handed to the local police station.
- Day lockers are emptied every night at 11:00pm. Their contents are treated as lost property.

## Claiming an item
Ask at the front desk during staffed hours and describe the item; valuables need photo ID to collect. You can also report a lost item with the Lost property form in the app and we'll notify you if it turns up.

## Responsibility
IronPulse is not responsible for items left unattended or in day lockers. Please use a padlock (sold at the front desk for $6).
