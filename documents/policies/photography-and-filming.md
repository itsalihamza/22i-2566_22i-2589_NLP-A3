# Photography and Filming Policy
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

## Filming yourself
You may film or photograph your own workout for personal use (for example to check your lifting technique), as long as no other member is identifiable in the shot.

## Not allowed
- Filming or photographing other members or staff without their clear permission.
- Any photography or filming in the changing rooms, showers or sauna — this leads to immediate cancellation of membership.
- Setting up tripods in walkways or on the cardio floor; use the marked filming spots next to the lifting platforms.

## Commercial content
Influencer, brand or professional shoots need written permission from the gym manager at least 7 days in advance (email hello@ironpulsefitness.example) and usually take place outside peak hours.

## Classes
Instructors may take photos of classes for IronPulse social media only after announcing it; tell the instructor if you don't want to appear.
