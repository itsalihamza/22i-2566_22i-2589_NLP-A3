# Access Card and QR Entry
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

## Getting in
Members enter with either the IronPulse access card or the QR code in the member app. Both work the same way at the door scanner.

## Cards
- Your first access card is free, issued at the front desk when you join (photo taken for the member record).
- Lost or damaged cards are replaced for $10 at the front desk. Report a lost card in the app straight away so it can be blocked.
- The QR code works as a free backup while you wait for a new card.

## Personal use only
Cards and QR codes are personal and cannot be shared. The door system flags two entries on the same card within 10 minutes; a shared card leads to suspension of the membership.

## When the scanner won't let you in
Common reasons: arriving outside your plan's access hours (Basic and Student: staffed hours only), a frozen or suspended membership, or an unpaid balance. Ask at the front desk or check Account in the app.
