# Cancellation Policy
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

IronPulse memberships have no minimum contract, but cancelling needs notice.

## How to cancel
- Give 30 days' written notice: fill in the cancellation form at the front desk, or email membership@ironpulsefitness.example from the email address on your account.
- Cancelling by phone, in the app chat, or by cancelling your direct debit with the bank is not accepted as notice.
- You will receive a confirmation email with your last day of membership within 2 working days.

## Payments and refunds
- Your last payment covers the 30-day notice period, so you may pay for one more month after giving notice.
- There is no refund for the current month.
- Unused personal training sessions are not refunded and stay valid until the pack expires.

## Consider a freeze instead
If you need a break for travel, work or a short injury, a freeze may suit you better: up to 3 months per year for $5 per month, with your price locked. For injury or illness, a medical freeze has no fee.

## Rejoining
You are welcome back at any time. The $20 joining fee applies again, at the current plan prices.

## New members
If you joined less than 7 days ago, see the Cooling-Off Period policy.
