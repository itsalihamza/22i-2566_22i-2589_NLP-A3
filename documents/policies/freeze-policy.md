# Membership Freeze Policy
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

A freeze pauses your membership for a while (for example travel or a busy period) without cancelling it.

## The rules
- You can freeze for up to 3 months per calendar year, in blocks of whole months (1, 2 or 3 months).
- A freeze is not free: while frozen you pay $5 per month instead of your normal plan price.
- Request it at least 7 days before the freeze should start, at the front desk or with the Freeze form in the member app.
- Freezes start on the 1st of a month and end on the last day of a month.

## While your membership is frozen
- Your access card is deactivated and class bookings are cancelled.
- Your price is locked: you return at the same monthly price you paid before the freeze.
- Plus and Elite members keep their personal locker during the freeze.
- Personal training packs pause too, and their validity is extended by the frozen time.

## Ending early
You can unfreeze early by asking at the front desk. Your normal plan price starts again from the next billing date (the 1st).

## Longer breaks
If you need more than 3 months off, you can cancel instead (30 days' written notice) and rejoin later; the $20 joining fee applies again when you rejoin. For injuries or illness, see the Medical Freeze policy.
