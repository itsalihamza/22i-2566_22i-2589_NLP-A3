# Cooling-Off Period for New Members
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

New members can change their mind shortly after joining.

## The rule
- If you cancel within 7 days of signing up, and you have visited the gym no more than 2 times, you get a full refund of your first month's membership fee.
- The $20 joining fee is not refunded.
- The 30-day notice period does not apply during the cooling-off period.

## How to use it
Tell the front desk in person or email membership@ironpulsefitness.example within the 7 days. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 working days.

## After 7 days
The standard Cancellation Policy applies: 30 days' written notice and no refund for the current month.
