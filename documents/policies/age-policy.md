# Age Policy
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

## Who can join
- 18 and over: standard membership on any plan.
- 16–17: can join any plan with a parent or guardian's waiver, signed in person at the front desk with photo ID for both.
- Under 16: cannot use the gym floor, classes or sauna, and cannot visit as a guest.

## Extra rules for 16–17 year olds
- No access during unstaffed 24/7 hours, even on the Plus or Elite plan. Access is limited to staffed hours until the member turns 18.
- No sauna access until 18, including on the Elite plan.
- They must complete a free intro session before training alone.

## Turning 18
24/7 access and sauna access (for Elite) are switched on automatically from the member's 18th birthday.
