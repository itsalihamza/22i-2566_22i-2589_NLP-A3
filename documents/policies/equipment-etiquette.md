# Equipment Etiquette and Gym Floor Rules
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

## Clean as you go
Wipe down benches, machines, mats and handles after use with the spray and paper towels at each station.

## Put it back
Re-rack plates, dumbbells and kettlebells after every exercise. Don't leave plates on bars or leg press machines.

## Cardio time limits
During peak hours (Monday–Friday 5:00pm–8:00pm), cardio machines are limited to 30 minutes when others are waiting.

## Lifting platforms and chalk
- Chalk is allowed on the lifting platforms only; use the chalk bowl, not your own loose chalk.
- Controlled drops of bumper plates are allowed on platforms only. Never drop dumbbells or iron plates.
- Use collars on loaded barbells.

## Squat racks
Racks are for exercises that need the rack (squats, presses, rack pulls). Bicep curls in the squat rack are not allowed during peak hours.

## Faulty equipment
Report broken or unsafe equipment to staff or with the Report form in the app, and put an "Out of order" tag on it (tags are at the front desk).
