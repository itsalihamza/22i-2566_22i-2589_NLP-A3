# Medical Freeze
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

Members who can't train because of injury, illness, surgery or pregnancy can apply for a medical freeze instead of a standard freeze.

## How it differs from a standard freeze
- Length: up to 6 months (a standard freeze is up to 3 months per year).
- Cost: no fee — the $5 monthly freeze fee is not charged.
- Notice: no 7-day notice needed; it can start from the date on your doctor's note, backdated by up to 14 days.

## What you need
- A doctor's or physiotherapist's note stating that you should not exercise and for roughly how long.
- Hand it in at the front desk or upload it with the Freeze form in the member app. Notes are seen only by the membership manager and deleted once the freeze ends.

## Coming back
- Your membership restarts at the same monthly price.
- We recommend booking a free return-to-training check with a trainer (Priya Nair specialises in post-rehabilitation exercise).
- Some conditions need written medical clearance before you return (see Health, Safety and Medical Clearance).
