# Changing Your Plan: Upgrades and Downgrades
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

## Upgrading (e.g. Basic to Plus, Plus to Elite)
- Takes effect immediately, at the front desk or in the member app (Account → Change plan).
- You pay the difference for the rest of the current month, worked out per day. Example: upgrading from Plus ($49) to Elite ($79) on the 16th of a 30-day month costs about $15 extra for that month.
- New benefits (24/7 access, classes, sauna, guest passes) work the same day. Elite guest passes are given in full for the month you upgrade.
- No new joining fee.

## Downgrading (e.g. Elite to Plus, Plus to Basic)
- Takes effect from the next billing month (the 1st). There is no refund for the current month.
- Request it at least 7 days before the 1st.
- Class bookings that are no longer included are cancelled when the downgrade starts. If you downgrade from Plus or Elite to Basic, your personal locker must be emptied by the last day of the month.

## Moving to the Student plan
Requires a valid student ID and starts from the next billing month.
