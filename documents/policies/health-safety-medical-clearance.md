# Health, Safety and Medical Clearance
*Category: Policies · Last updated: September 2026*

## Health questionnaire
Every new member and first-time guest completes a short health questionnaire (PAR-Q) when signing up.

## When medical clearance is needed
If you answer "yes" to any of the questions below, you need written clearance from a doctor before training:
- a heart condition, chest pain during exercise, or fainting/dizziness during exercise in the past 12 months;
- surgery in the past 3 months;
- pregnancy (clearance is also needed before joining classes);
- a condition your doctor has told you limits exercise.
Until the clearance is received you can tour the gym and meet a trainer, but not train.

## Injuries at the gym
- All staff hold first-aid and CPR certificates. An AED (defibrillator) is next to the front desk and a second one is by the lifting platforms.
- Report any injury to staff straight away; staff complete an incident report.
- IronPulse staff and trainers do not diagnose or treat injuries. For pain or injury, see a doctor or physiotherapist.

## Hydration and illness
Water fountains are on the gym floor and in the studios. Please don't train with a fever or a contagious illness.
