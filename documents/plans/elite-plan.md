# Elite Membership Plan
*Category: Membership plans · Last updated: September 2026*

Elite is IronPulse's all-inclusive membership.

## Price
- $79 per month, plus the one-time $20 joining fee.

## What's included
- Everything in Plus, including 24/7 access and a permanent locker.
- Unlimited group classes, booked up to 7 days ahead.
- Two guest passes per month. Guest passes reset on the 1st of each month and do not roll over.
- Sauna access (6:00am–9:00pm daily, see the Sauna Rules).
- Towel service: a fresh towel at the front desk on every visit.
- Priority waitlist: when a class is full, Elite members are placed ahead of Plus members on the waitlist.

## Fair use for unlimited classes
To keep classes available for everyone, an Elite member who has three no-shows within 30 days cannot book classes for 7 days. Cancelling at least 2 hours before the class is never counted as a no-show.

## Who it suits
Members who take two or more classes a week, bring friends, or want the sauna and towel service. If you take only one class a week, Plus is cheaper.
