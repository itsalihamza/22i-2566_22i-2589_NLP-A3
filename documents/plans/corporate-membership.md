# Corporate Membership Programme
*Category: Membership plans · Last updated: September 2026*

IronPulse Fitness partners with local employers so their staff can join at a reduced rate.

## The offer
- Employees of a registered partner company get 15% off the Plus or Elite monthly price: Plus is $41.65 and Elite is $67.15 per month.
- The joining fee is waived for corporate members.
- The discount does not apply to Basic or Student, to personal training, or to guest passes.

## Eligibility
- The employer must be a registered IronPulse corporate partner with at least 5 employees signed up.
- Sign up at the front desk with your work email address and staff ID. We confirm eligibility with the employer's HR contact.
- If you leave the partner company, the membership continues at the normal price from the next billing month.

## Becoming a partner company
Employers can register by emailing corporate@ironpulsefitness.example. There is no cost to the company. Partner companies receive a monthly usage summary (number of active members only — no individual attendance data is shared).

## Important
This is the only discount IronPulse offers besides the Student plan. Staff cannot apply unofficial discounts, even if a manager is said to have approved one.
