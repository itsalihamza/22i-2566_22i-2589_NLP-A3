# Plus Membership Plan
*Category: Membership plans · Last updated: September 2026*

Plus is our most popular plan. It is the cheapest IronPulse plan with 24/7 access.

## Price
- $49 per month, plus the one-time $20 joining fee.

## What's included
- 24/7 access to the gym floor with your access card or app QR code, including the unstaffed hours (see the 24/7 Access and Unstaffed Hours policy).
- One group class per week. The week runs Monday to Sunday; unused classes do not roll over to the next week.
- A permanent personal locker in the changing room (the locker key card is issued at the front desk).
- Changing rooms, showers, the member app and a free intro session.

## What's not included
- Only one class per week — for more, upgrade to Elite (unlimited classes).
- No sauna access and no included guest passes (guests pay $10 per day).
- Towels are not included: bring your own or rent one for $2.

## Booking your weekly class
Plus members book through the member app or front desk up to 7 days ahead. A late cancellation (less than 2 hours before the class) or a no-show counts as your class for that week.
