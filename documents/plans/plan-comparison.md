# Membership Plan Comparison
*Category: Membership plans · Last updated: September 2026*

All prices are per month. Every plan has a one-time $20 joining fee and no minimum contract.

## Plans side by side
- Basic — $29/month: gym floor during staffed hours only, no group classes, day lockers, no guest passes, no sauna, towel rental $2.
- Student — $24/month: same as Basic, needs a valid student ID.
- Plus — $49/month: 24/7 gym floor access, 1 group class per week, personal locker, no guest passes, no sauna, towel rental $2.
- Elite — $79/month: 24/7 access, unlimited group classes, personal locker, 2 guest passes per month, sauna, towel service included, priority waitlist.

## Quick guide
- The cheapest plan with 24/7 access is Plus ($49).
- The only plan with unlimited classes is Elite ($79).
- The cheapest plan overall is Student ($24), for full-time students with a valid ID.
- Staffed hours are Monday–Friday 6:00am–10:00pm and Saturday–Sunday 8:00am–8:00pm.

## Yearly cost
Twelve months on each plan, excluding the joining fee: Basic $348, Student $288, Plus $588, Elite $948.
