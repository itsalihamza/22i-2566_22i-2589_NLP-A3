# Student Membership Plan
*Category: Membership plans · Last updated: September 2026*

The Student plan gives full-time students the Basic membership at a lower price.

## Price
- $24 per month.
- The $20 joining fee still applies.

## Eligibility
- You must show a valid student ID from a university, college or high school (age 16+, see the Age Policy) when you sign up.
- Student status is re-checked every September. If you can't show a current student ID by 30 September, the membership automatically moves to the Basic plan at $29 per month from the next billing month.

## What's included
Exactly the same as the Basic plan:
- Gym floor access during staffed hours only (Monday–Friday 6:00am–10:00pm, Saturday–Sunday 8:00am–8:00pm).
- Changing rooms, showers, day lockers, the member app, and one free intro session.

## What's not included
- No group classes, no 24/7 access, no sauna, no guest passes.
- Students who want classes can upgrade to Plus or Elite at the normal price; there is no student version of those plans.
