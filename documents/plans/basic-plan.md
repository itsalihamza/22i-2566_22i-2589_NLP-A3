# Basic Membership Plan
*Category: Membership plans · Last updated: September 2026*

The Basic plan is IronPulse Fitness's entry-level membership for people who want access to the gym floor and train on their own.

## Price
- $29 per month, billed on the 1st of each month.
- A one-time $20 joining fee is paid with the first month.
- No minimum contract: cancel any time with 30 days' written notice.

## What's included
- Full access to the gym floor (free weights, machines, cardio area, stretching zone) during staffed hours only: Monday–Friday 6:00am–10:00pm, Saturday–Sunday 8:00am–8:00pm.
- Use of changing rooms, showers and day lockers (bring your own padlock).
- One free 30-minute intro session with a trainer in your first 30 days.
- The IronPulse member app for entry by QR code.

## What's not included
- Group classes are not included. Basic members cannot book classes; upgrade to Plus (one class per week) or Elite (unlimited classes) to join them.
- No 24/7 access — the door scanner only admits Basic members during staffed hours.
- No sauna access and no guest passes (guests pay the $10 guest day pass).

## Who it suits
Members who train during the day or evening, already know their way around a gym, and don't need classes.
