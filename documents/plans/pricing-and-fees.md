# Price List and Fees
*Category: Membership plans · Last updated: September 2026*

A complete list of IronPulse Fitness charges in one place.

## Memberships
- Per month: Basic $29, Student $24, Plus $49, Elite $79.
- Joining fee (one time, all plans): $20 — waived only for corporate partner members.

## Visits without a membership
- Guest day pass (accompanied by a member): $10.
- Non-member day pass (no member needed): $15, valid during staffed hours only, photo ID required.

## Personal training
- Single 60-minute session: $55.
- 5-session pack: $250 (valid 3 months).
- 10-session pack: $460 (valid 6 months).

## Extras
- Towel rental: $2 (included for Elite).
- Replacement access card: $10 (first card free).
- Padlock for day lockers: $6.
- Boxing hand wraps: $8 (gloves can be borrowed free).

## Account charges
- Membership freeze: $5 per month while frozen (no charge for a medical freeze with a doctor's note).
- Failed payment fee: $10, charged only if the retry also fails.

## Payment methods
Monthly memberships are paid by direct debit or by a debit/credit card on file. Day passes, towels, padlocks and wraps can be paid by card or cash at the front desk.
