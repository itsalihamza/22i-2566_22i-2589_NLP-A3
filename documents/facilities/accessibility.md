# Accessibility
*Category: Facilities · Last updated: September 2026*

IronPulse aims to be usable by everyone.

## Building
- Step-free entrance with an automatic door from the plaza, and two accessible parking bays next to the plaza entrance.
- All gym areas are on the ground floor; there are no stairs inside the gym.
- An accessible changing room with a shower seat, grab rails and an emergency pull cord.

## Equipment
- 3 cable machines and 2 upper-body cardio machines (arm ergometers) that can be used from a wheelchair.
- Adjustable benches and height-adjustable racks.

## Support
- Registered assistance dogs are welcome (not in the sauna or showers).
- A carer or support worker accompanying a member trains free of charge; register them at the front desk.
- Staff can give a guided tour and an extended intro session (up to 60 minutes instead of 30) on request.
