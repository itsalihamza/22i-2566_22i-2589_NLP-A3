# Gym Floor and Equipment
*Category: Facilities · Last updated: September 2026*

The gym floor is about 900 square metres and is open to every plan during that plan's access hours.

## Strength area
- 6 squat racks with safety arms and 4 lifting platforms with bumper plates.
- Dumbbells from 2kg to 50kg, kettlebells from 4kg to 40kg.
- 2 cable crossover stations, a Smith machine, leg press, hack squat and 14 selectorised machines.

## Cardio area
- 12 treadmills, 8 bikes, 6 cross-trainers, 4 rowers, 3 stair climbers and 4 assault bikes.
- 30-minute limit at peak times (Monday–Friday 5:00pm–8:00pm) when others are waiting.

## Functional zone
Turf lane for sled pushes, battle ropes, plyo boxes, medicine balls and a rig for pull-ups and suspension trainers.

## What IronPulse does not have
No swimming pool, no squash or tennis courts, no reformer Pilates machines, and no climbing wall.
