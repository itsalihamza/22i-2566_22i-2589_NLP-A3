# Opening Hours
*Category: Facilities · Last updated: September 2026*

## Staffed hours
- Monday–Friday: 6:00am–10:00pm
- Saturday–Sunday: 8:00am–8:00pm
During staffed hours the front desk, studios, sauna (6:00am–9:00pm only) and shop are open and staff are on the gym floor.

## 24/7 access
Plus and Elite members aged 18+ can use the gym floor, changing rooms and showers at any time with their access card or app QR code. See the 24/7 Access and Unstaffed Hours policy.

## Who can come when
- Basic and Student members, guests and day-pass visitors: staffed hours only.
- Plus and Elite members: any time.

## Busiest times
The gym floor is busiest Monday–Thursday 5:00pm–8:00pm. The quietest staffed times are 10:00am–3:00pm on weekdays and after 6:00pm on Sundays.

## Public holidays
Opening hours change on public holidays — see Holiday Hours and Closures.
