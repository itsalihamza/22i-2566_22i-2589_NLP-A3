# Holiday Hours and Closures
*Category: Facilities · Last updated: September 2026*

## Fully closed
- Christmas Day (25 December) and New Year's Day (1 January): the building is closed to everyone, including 24/7 access.

## Other public holidays
- Staffed hours are reduced to 8:00am–2:00pm.
- There are no group classes on public holidays. Bookings are not possible for those dates.
- 24/7 access for Plus and Elite members continues as normal outside the reduced staffed hours.

## Planned maintenance
- Each year the studio floors and the sauna are serviced during the first week of August. The sauna is closed for that week; the gym floor stays open.
- Any other planned closures are announced in the member app and by email at least 14 days in advance.

## Unexpected closures
Power cuts, severe weather or emergencies are announced in the app as soon as possible. Classes cancelled by IronPulse never count against a Plus member's weekly class.
