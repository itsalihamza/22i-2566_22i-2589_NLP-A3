# Recovery Zone
*Category: Facilities · Last updated: September 2026*

A quiet area next to the studios for stretching and recovery, open to members on every plan during their access hours.

## Equipment
- Foam rollers, massage balls, stretching straps and mats.
- 2 percussion massage guns, borrowed from the front desk with your access card (staffed hours only, 15 minutes each).
- A compression boots station for Elite members (book a 20-minute slot in the app).

## Rules
- No heavy training in the recovery zone — it's for stretching and mobility only.
- Keep voices low and phones on silent.
- Wipe down mats and rollers after use.

## Guided mobility
Sam Rivera's Restorative Yoga class (Sunday 10:00am) is the best class for recovery. For one-to-one mobility work, book a personal training session with Sam or Priya Nair.
