# Changing Rooms, Lockers and Showers
*Category: Facilities · Last updated: September 2026*

## Changing rooms
Men's, women's and two private gender-neutral changing rooms, each with showers, toilets, hairdryers and a vanity area. Shampoo and body wash dispensers are in every shower.

## Day lockers (all plans)
- Free to use while you are in the building; bring your own padlock or buy one at the front desk for $6.
- Day lockers are emptied every night at 11:00pm and the contents go to lost property.

## Personal lockers (Plus and Elite)
- A permanent locker kept for you for as long as you are a Plus or Elite member, including during a freeze.
- The locker key card is issued at the front desk; a lost locker key card costs $10 to replace.
- If you downgrade to Basic or cancel, empty your locker by your last day of membership.

## Towels
Bring your own, rent one for $2 at the front desk, or use the towel service included with Elite.
