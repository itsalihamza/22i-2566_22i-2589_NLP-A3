# Sauna Rules
*Category: Facilities · Last updated: September 2026*

## Who can use it
- Elite members aged 18+, and guests accompanying an Elite member.
- Basic, Student and Plus members do not have sauna access. A one-off sauna session is not sold separately.

## Hours
6:00am–9:00pm every day. The sauna is closed during unstaffed hours and for one week each August for servicing.

## Rules
- Maximum 15 minutes per session, then cool down before going back in.
- Swimwear or a towel wrap must be worn; sit on a towel.
- Maximum 8 people at a time.
- No phones, no glass bottles and no essential oils on the stones.
- Shower before entering.

## Health
Don't use the sauna if you are pregnant, have heart or blood pressure problems, or have been drinking alcohol, unless your doctor has said it is safe. Leave immediately if you feel dizzy or unwell and tell a staff member.
