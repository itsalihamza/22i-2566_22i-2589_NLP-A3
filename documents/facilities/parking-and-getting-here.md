# Parking and Getting Here
*Category: Facilities · Last updated: September 2026*

## Car parking
- IronPulse members park free for up to 2 hours in the Ridgeway Plaza car park. Validate your ticket on the scanner at the IronPulse front desk (during unstaffed hours, scan your app QR code at the car park exit instead).
- After 2 hours the plaza's normal parking charges apply.
- Area B of the car park is closed on Saturdays 8:45am–10:15am for the Bootcamp class.
- Two accessible parking bays are next to the plaza entrance.

## Public transport
- Bus routes 7 and 19 stop at "Foundry Lane", about 2 minutes' walk away.
- Riverside train station is a 12-minute walk.

## Bikes
A covered bike rack with 20 spaces is outside the entrance and covered by CCTV. Bring your own lock.

## Electric vehicles
Four EV charging points are on level 1 of the plaza car park (paid separately through the plaza).
