# Wi-Fi and Other Amenities
*Category: Facilities · Last updated: September 2026*

## Wi-Fi
Free Wi-Fi on the network "IronPulse-Members". The password changes monthly and is printed on the front desk board and shown in the app under Info.

## Water and drinks
- Filtered water fountains on the gym floor, in the studios and in the recovery zone. Bring a refillable bottle.
- The front desk shop sells bottled water, protein shakes and energy drinks (card or cash).

## Shop
Padlocks ($6), boxing hand wraps ($8), grip socks, towels and IronPulse-branded clothing.

## Reception lounge
Seating, phone charging points and a notice board for community events. The lounge is open during staffed hours.

## Not available
There is no café, no crèche, and no laundry service.
